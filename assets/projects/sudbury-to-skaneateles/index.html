Why this one is different

No backtracking, and the main building at both ends

Every other arrangement of these three inns runs the Sudbury leg twice — out to Boston's western suburbs and straight back to the Berkshires. Putting the Wayside first turns the trip into a single westward line, and it is the only shape that still lands the Skaneateles night inside the Sherwood Inn itself rather than one of its village houses.

Book Roar Longer, not the New York rate

Roar Longer saves $335 over We Love NY! on this stay, and $390 over the best available rate. The New York discount only covers Sunday through Thursday nights, and two of these three are a Friday and a Saturday — so it drops from 20% to about 3% in practice. This is the one leg of the whole trip where the residency discount is not the right answer.

What it costs, and what it trades

All-in, taxes and fees included

$2,450–2,480 all in on Roar Longer, against $2,785–2,815 if you booked the New York rate instead.

It is the most expensive way to run this trip. The Red Lion prices 1–4 October at $608 a night against $274–304 earlier in the week — peak Berkshires foliage weekend. Roar Longer narrows that gap but does not close it.

The two-night Red Lion stay becomes three. The separate single night after the Wayside disappears; you unpack once and stay put.

No leg is driven twice. Sudbury, then Stockbridge, then Skaneateles — each further west, ending nearest to New York.

Both main buildings are held: the Red Lion's Main Inn for all three nights, and the Sherwood Inn proper on the last.

No suite is required. A standard Main Inn Queen Room covers all three nights; a Main Inn Queen Suite is offered at $631 on Roar Longer if you would prefer one.

The bathtub still has to be arranged by phone

Neither the Red Lion's booking engine nor its website distinguishes tub from shower. The Room Amenities panel is empty for every room type, and all eight Main Inn types are described identically as “a private bath.” The inn also states that each of its 82 rooms is individually decorated and that images may not reflect the room assigned — so tub presence varies within a room type.

Book the three nights online, then call (413) 298-5545 and have a tub noted on the reservation. The Wayside commits in writing: every room there has a full bath.
