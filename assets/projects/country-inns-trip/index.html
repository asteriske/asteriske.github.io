What the Wayside date decides

One fixed night, and everything else follows from it

With the Wayside pinned to Wednesday 30 September, the two-night Red Lion stay can only be Monday 28 – Wednesday 30, and the single Red Lion night after it can only be Thursday 1 October. All three of those nights have Main Inn rooms open, so the Red Lion requirement holds comfortably. That leaves one question: which end of the trip the Skaneateles night sits on.

Placed at the end, the Skaneateles night falls on Friday 2 October — a night the Sherwood's main building is closed. Its village houses are open.

Placed at the front, it falls on Sunday 27 September, when the Sherwood's main building has eleven rooms open from $299.

Moving that one night to the front is the only change needed. The Red Lion → Wayside → Red Lion sequence stays exactly as you laid it out.

It also runs the route cleanly west to east, rather than closing with a 250-mile drive back across New York.

The bathtub has to be arranged by phone

You asked for a tub on the two-night Red Lion stay, and that is the one thing none of these booking engines will tell you. The Red Lion's room-details panel has a Room Amenities section that is completely empty for every room type, and its website describes all eight Main Inn types identically as “a private bath” — no mention of tub or shower anywhere on either site.

There is a structural reason, too. The Red Lion states that each of its 82 rooms is individually decorated and that images “may not reflect the exact room and amenities assigned to guests,” so tub presence almost certainly varies within a room type. Booking a type would not secure a tub even if a photo showed one.

The two-night stay is Monday 28 – Wednesday 30 September in every option here, so this is one call regardless of which you pick: (413) 298-5545, and have a tub noted on the reservation. The Wayside is the one property that commits in writing — every room there has a “full bath.”

What the numbers include

Verified against each property

Red Lion: a $25.00 per-night Preservation Fee plus 11.7% Massachusetts lodging tax, both folded into every total shown.

Sherwood group: a flat 20.0% New York lodging tax on the Skaneateles night.

Wayside: quoted rates are pre-tax; the inn publishes $220–250 double occupancy and does not list its tax rate. Massachusetts lodging tax runs 11.7% in Stockbridge, and Sudbury's local rate is unconfirmed — budget roughly $25–30 on top.

Breakfast for two in the Old Tap Room comes with the Wayside stay. Wednesday 30 September is a full-rate night, so no kitchen-closure reduction applies.

We Love NY! gives New York residents 20% off, Sunday through Thursday nights only. Every Red Lion night in the first two options falls inside that window.

Roar Longer takes 10% off a two-night stay with no residency test — useful if the NY rate does not apply, though it lands above the NY rate wherever both are offered.

Friday 2 and Saturday 3 October each carry a two-night minimum at the Red Lion, which is what forces the third option into a single three-night block.

The Sherwood's main building is open on 24, 27, 28 and 29 September and on 1, 4, 5 and 6 October, and closed on the nights between — including 2 October.

Booking the Wayside

Telephone only · Wed 30 September

The Wayside takes no online reservations. The website offers an inquiry form that explicitly does not hold a room. Call the front desk at 978-443-1776 ×1.

There are ten guest rooms in total. Rooms 9 and 10 date to the late 18th century and are noted as booking first.

Every room has a full bath, king, queen, or full beds, and all queens are four-poster canopies. No pets.

Check-in 3:00 pm, check-out 11:00 am, and 72 hours' notice to cancel. With one night available, this is the reservation to secure first.
