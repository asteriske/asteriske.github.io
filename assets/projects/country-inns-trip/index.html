The bathtub has to be arranged by phone

You asked for a tub on the two-night Red Lion stay, and that is the one thing none of these booking engines will tell you. The Red Lion's room-details panel has a Room Amenities section that is completely empty for every room type, and its website describes all eight Main Inn types identically as “a private bath” — no mention of tub or shower anywhere on either site.

There is a structural reason, too. The Red Lion states that each of its 82 rooms is individually decorated and that images “may not reflect the exact room and amenities assigned to guests,” so tub presence almost certainly varies within a room type. Booking a type would not secure a tub even if a photo showed one.

Book whichever itinerary you like online, then call (413) 298-5545 and have a tub noted on the reservation. The Wayside is the one property that commits in writing — every room there has a “full bath.”

What the numbers include

Verified against each property

Red Lion: a $25.00 per-night Preservation Fee plus 11.7% Massachusetts lodging tax, both folded into every total shown.

Sherwood group: a flat 20.0% New York lodging tax, applied to Skaneateles nights.

Wayside: quoted rates are pre-tax; the inn publishes $220–250 double occupancy and does not list its tax rate. Massachusetts lodging tax runs 11.7% in Stockbridge, and Sudbury's local rate is unconfirmed — budget roughly $25–30 on top.

Breakfast for two in the Old Tap Room comes with every Wayside stay, except after Sunday and Monday nights, when the kitchen is closed and the rate drops $20.

We Love NY! gives New York residents 20% off, Sunday through Thursday nights only. It is the strongest public discount at the Red Lion on these dates.

Roar Longer takes 10% off a two-night stay without a residency test — useful if the NY rate does not apply, though it lands above the NY rate wherever both are offered.

Friday 2 October carries a two-night minimum at the Red Lion, which is what fixes the check-in window at Saturday through Monday.

Sherwood Inn's main building has one room open across this window — Room 29 on Thursday 1 October. Its sister houses have wide availability throughout.

Booking the Wayside

Telephone only

The Wayside takes no online reservations. The website offers an inquiry form that explicitly does not hold a room. Call the front desk at 978-443-1776 ×1.

There are ten guest rooms in total. Rooms 9 and 10 date to the late 18th century and are noted as booking first.

Every room has a full bath, king, queen, or full beds, and all queens are four-poster canopies. No pets.

Check-in 3:00 pm, check-out 11:00 am, and 72 hours' notice to cancel. Availability for these dates can only be confirmed by phone.
